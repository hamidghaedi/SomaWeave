- Dates: all 18 specimen dates have month precision; the only treatment (scenario04) also has month-precision start/end. No mixed day/month/year case, overlapping date bounds, date-missing specimen or missing treatment-date case is supplied.
- Consequences: nine small-variant findings supply a consequence and derive a coarse class; the CNA/fusion findings have no supplied consequence and derive no class. No small-variant consequence-missing case, independent `consequence_class` input field, transcript-specific splice disagreement or unknown exon membership is covered.
- Evidence: no nonempty `conditions` arrays and no AND/OR compound molecular profiles occur. Scenario08 contains separate incompatible fusion/expression records, which do not exercise compound-profile satisfaction.
- Specimens: one FFPE tumour and one blood normal per scenario; no multiple tumour time points, differing preservation or missing normal specimen. No structural breakpoint, measured RNA-fusion evidence, quantitative amplification segment, or signature result is provided.
- Genomic identity: all 11 findings lack GRCh38 nucleotide coordinates. Protein HGVS does not in general identify a unique genomic allele; the CNV/fusion labels alone cannot supply a minimal SNV/indel VCF. Mapping review is a separate owner decision.
